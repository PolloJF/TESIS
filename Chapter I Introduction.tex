\chapter{Introduction}\label{ch:intro}

Primarily, this thesis adresses the development of a complete Through-the-Earth communication system for underground environments. The need for reliable communication has become increasingly important in recent years, particularly in the mining and construction industries, especially in situations such as mine emergencies, where communication is critical for worker safety and operational efficiency. Underground environments present unique challenges for communication systems, including the presence of conductive materials, complex geological structures, and limited line-of-sight propagation. These challenges can lead to significant signal attenuation and interference, making it difficult to establish reliable communication links. Traditional communication methods, such as radio and wired systems, can operate in normal conditions, but they often fail in extreme environments or material collapse. As a result, there is a growing need for innovative communication solutions that can overcome these challenges and provide reliable connectivity in underground environments.
For this reason, the Through the Earth (TTE) communication system has been proposed as a potential solution for underground communication. Typically, TTE systems use low-frequency electromagnetic waves to transmit data through the earth, enabling communication over long distances and through various geological materials. This technology has the potential to revolutionize underground communication and improve safety in the mining and construction industries. However, developing a complete TTE communication system for underground environments requires a comprehensive understanding of the technology's underlying principles, challenges, and potential applications.

\section{Motivation}
The motivation is the need for a reliable communication system in underground environments without existing infrastructure. The possibility of communicating across hundreds of meters of rock and other solid material, with a simple system that can be implemented quickly and with limited resources, can be a game-changer in mining and other underground activities. The ability to establish communication links in challenging environments can enhance safety, improve operational efficiency, and facilitate emergency response efforts. Additionally, the development of a complete TTE communication system can contribute to the advancement of communication technologies and provide valuable insights into the challenges and solutions associated with underground environments.

\section{Hypothesis}
It is possible to develop a complete Through the Earth (TTE) communication system for underground environments, considering relatively simple hardware and limited software resources, resulting in a useful system to reach text messaging through hundreds of meters of rock and solid material.

\section{Objectives}

The objectives of this thesis are divided into two levels: general and specific objectives.
\subsection{General Objective}
The main objective of this thesis is to develop a complete TTE communication system for underground environments, focusing on the design, implementation, and evaluation of the system's components and performance. 
\subsection{Specific Objectives}

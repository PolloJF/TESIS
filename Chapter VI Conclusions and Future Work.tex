\section{Conclusions}
In conclusion, the development of MAGIC has demonstrated the feasibility of a complete Through the Earth (TTE) communication system for underground mining using relatively simple hardware and software components. The system has shown promising results, achieving text messaging through at least 200 meters of rock and solid material. MAGIC is a valuable tool for mining and other industries that need a reliable communication solution in challenging underground environments, considering its relatively low cost.
\newp
The iterative design and testing process allowed the optimization of the system's performance, finally reaching a 100\% detection rate, a data transmission rate of 8 bits per second (bps), considering a total of 256 characters coded by 16 tones. The SER (Symbol Error Rate) is lower than 5\%.
\newp
Regarding the system's performance in operational environments, it can be concluded that it works properly at Mina Benjamín at V region, especially detecting received messages. However, the demodulation performance is strongly affected by the noise spectral distribution in the mine, which is more variable and with higher amplitude compared to the noise at the National Astronomical Observatory in Cerro Calán. This type of interference, which is sudden and short-lived, particularly affects demodulator performance due to peak detection. The next step is to better understand the characteristics of the noise in the mine and other environments to adapt the system and subtract the mean spectral distribution of noise to improve SER.
\newp
Regarding the secondary objectives, these were met. Primarily, the design and implementation of resonant coils as antennas for TTE communication was successfully achieved. The coils tuned to 3.2 kHz have a diameter of 56 cm and an analog bandwidth of 450 Hz and can properly receive and transmit a modulated magnetic field with a mean current of 2 A. Additionally, signal processing techniques such as digital filtering, a custom correlation algorithm, and decimation were developed to enhance the reliability and efficiency of the system. Particularly, the \textit{correlation by parts} algorithm has demonstrated its effectiveness in several scenarios and with a natural dispersive medium, achieving successful detection of the signal at SNR near  -10 dB, considering only the system's band.
\newp
The noise has been measured in many locations and it was spectrally characterized, identifying the main interference sources as odd harmonics from the power grid.
\newp
Finally, the performance of the TTE communication system was evaluated in various underground conditions, including different geological materials and environmental factors, but it is necessary to continue testing the system in more challenging environments and with longer distances to better understand the limitations of the system and how to improve it.
\newp
Overall, the development of MAGIC represents a significant step forward in the field of TTE communication systems for underground environments. Particularly in Chile, it provides a foundation for future research and development to further enhance the performance and reliability of TTE communication systems in mining and other underground applications, improving safety and operational efficiency.

\section{Future Work}

There are several areas for future work to enhance the performance and reliability of the MAGIC system. These areas include:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Adaptive Thresholding and Noise Cancellation}
    \item \textbf{Channel Estimation}
    \item \textbf{Automatic Tunning}
    \item \textbf{Optimize Coupling Variability and Misalignment}
    \item \textbf{Automatic Switching and adaptation to underground environments}
\end{itemize}

After measurements were done in the mine, it is clear that the noise has a different spectral distribution compared to the astronomical observatory. Besides of knowing better the behavior of possible noise sources in VLF and ULF, that are primarly energy systems to substract their spectral influence digitally to improve the performance of the demodulator, it is possible to implement robust algorithms to mitigate the effect of impulsive noise in an adaptive way, for example using adaptive filters based on maximum correntropy criterion (MCC) \cite{wang2018adaptive} that is has demonstrated to be effective in mitigating impulsive noise in communication systems, specifically in VLF bands. Additionally, implement an adaptive thresholding technique that can adjust the detection threshold based on constant tests of communication and constant measures of correlation with noise, which could improve the performance of the system in changing noise conditions.
\newp
Channel estimation could be implemented to determine the effective gain of the channel and phase response to adapt the system accordingly, depending on its site and rock conditions. This could be achieved by measuring the transfer function of the channel as a previous calibration step, or by using pilot symbols during the communication process to continuously estimate the channel characteristics and adapt the system in real time.
\newp
Automatic tuning is key to implementing MAGIC in different environments and scenarios. In some places, to reach a greater distance, it could be necessary to deploy larger coils. To maintain MAGIC's portable characteristics, this coil has to be able to be disassembled and reassembled in a short time. For that reason, the design of the coil has to be optimized to be easily tunable by variable capacitors, because the natural inductance of the coil can change depending on its form. Additionally, the system could be designed to automatically adjust the tuning based on feedback from the received signal strength or quality and relatively clean bands of spectrum to set symbol frequencies, ensuring optimal performance in different environments. %Incluir referencia a analisis del ruido realizado en la seccion de analisis de resultados en la mina.
\newp
The coupling variability and misalignment are critical aspects to consider in future works. As it was explained in previous sections, the performance of the system can be significantly affected by misalignment between the transmitter and receiver coils, as well as variations in the coupling due to changes in the environment or movement of the coils. Future work could focus on developing techniques to optimize the coil design to minimize the effects of misalignment and coupling variability, such as using multiple coils in orthogonal planes to secure message detection and transmission. Finally, the system has to automatically switch between transmission and reception modes, taking advantage of series and parallel resonant modes of the coil to optimize the performance in each mode. In Figure \ref{new_schematic}, an electrical schematic of the system with the necessary components to implement this functionality is shown.

\begin{figure}
    \centering
    \includegraphics[width=0.8\textwidth]{img/diagrams/new.jpg}
    \caption{Electrical schematic of the system with components for automatic switching between transmission and reception modes.}
    \label{new_schematic}
\end{figure}

In the previous figure, the principal switch could be a multiple relay module that can switch the resonant coil terminals (in series or parallel) between the amplifier output and the audio interface input. This switch ideally has to be composed of solid-state relays due to electromagnetic compatibility issues; an electromechanical relay could not work properly due to the strong magnetic field generated by the proper coil in transmission mode.
\newp
The automatic version of MAGIC is currently being tested, considering the integration with a Raspberry Pi 500, regarding its GPIO pins and processing power, this device could control the switching of the coil, run the transmission and reception routines automatically.
\newp
The following step is to implement a more robust version of the system that can operate in hostile environments. In mining, for example, the system has to function properly under harsh conditions such as high temperatures, humidity, dust, and vibrations. To achieve this, the hardware will be redesigned with ruggedized components and enclosures.

% RESUMEN O ABSTRACT
\begin{abstractd}
	This document presents the development of a complete Through the Earth (TTE) communication system designed for underground environments. The system called MAGIC \textit(MAGnetic Induction Communication) utilizes low-frequency magnetic induction and non-coherent M-FSK modulation to enable reliable communication through solid materials such as rock and soil. The thesis covers the design, implementation, and evaluation of the system's components, including resonant coils, signal processing techniques, and communication protocols. Experimental results demonstrate the system's ability to achieve text messaging over distances up to 200 meters in underground conditions, with a symbol error rate close to 5\%, highlighting its potential applications in mining and other underground industries. The findings contribute to the advancement of TTE communication technologies and provide insights into overcoming the challenges associated with underground communication.
\end{abstractd}
